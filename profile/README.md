# Addady

> **End-to-End Utility Data Platform | Automated Metering, Payment Infrastructure & Business Intelligence**

From applying for a meter, to verified AI readings, to paying for exactly what you consume. One ecosystem for electricity, water & gas.

Addady connects citizens, businesses, and utility providers on a single live data stream. AI-powered OCR converts meter photos into verified readings, machine-learning models screen every submission for fraud and anomalies, and KYC-verified meters keep ownership records clear. Bills are settled in-app, and provider teams operate the entire cycle through management dashboards with holding-group analytics. The result is billing built on accurate, validated data rather than estimates, manual entry, and field visits.

## Empowering Technology

We believe the technology we build should empower the wider engineering community, not just our own platform. That is why we open-sourced [Sentinel](https://github.com/Addady/sentinel), our agentic end-to-end QA framework for testing across every surface of a stack: API, database, web, mail, and mobile.
